# Governance

Mycelium governance is designed for local legitimacy and federated coordination.

## Goals

- Keep decision-making close to affected communities
- Provide transparent change management for protocol and policy
- Support plural governance models across regions while preserving interoperability

## Governance domains

- **Protocol governance** — specs, compatibility, and RFC decisions
- **Operational governance** — node operation policy, local deployment rules
- **Data governance** — consent frameworks and access boundaries
- **Safety governance** — incident response, security advisories, and rollback decisions

## Decision process (initial)

1. Proposal opened as RFC
2. Public review period
3. Local and federation impact assessment
4. Maintainer and community ratification
5. Staged rollout with rollback criteria

## Non-negotiables

- No mass-surveillance feature paths
- No non-consensual extraction of community data
- Respect for indigenous and local data sovereignty
