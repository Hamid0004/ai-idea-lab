# Principles

1. **Life first**
   - Technology serves ecosystems and communities before institutions or extraction.

2. **Local autonomy**
   - Data and decisions stay near their source whenever possible.

3. **Resilience over efficiency**
   - Prefer robust and recoverable systems over brittle optimization.

4. **Transparency**
   - Open protocol, open governance, and inspectable implementation pathways.

5. **Privacy by architecture**
   - Make surveillance structurally difficult through protocol and system design.

6. **Consent by default**
   - Sharing is negotiated and policy-driven; raw sensitive data is minimized.
