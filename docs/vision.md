# Vision

Mycelium is a decentralized, self-evolving digital organism for planetary resilience.

It prioritizes local intelligence, offline-first operation, and consent by design. Rather than extracting centralized value from communities and ecosystems, it supports place-based autonomy and cooperative stewardship.

## Long-term direction

- Build a globally interoperable, locally governed mesh of intelligent nodes.
- Ensure operation under constrained, disconnected, or degraded environments.
- Enable community ownership of infrastructure, policy, and lifecycle.
- Treat privacy as a systems property, not a legal checkbox.
